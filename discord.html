CT Community

What happens here

  • Release announcements for CT Client and CT Loader updates.
  • Setup help for installation, version selection, and Microsoft sign-in.
  • Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge profile questions, including Fabric mod routing for Sodium and similar mods.
  • CT-native mod development: the ct.mod.json format, entrypoints, and the loader API.
  • General Minecraft Java chat with other CT users.

Before you post

  • Check the setup guide, settings reference, and docs first; many questions are answered there.
  • Include your CT version, operating system, and the Minecraft version you selected.
  • Paste the relevant lines from the CT output window and, for launch failures, the end of <game directory>/logs/ct-client-launch.log.

Security: never share Microsoft account credentials, access tokens, or device codes. CT staff will never ask for them.
